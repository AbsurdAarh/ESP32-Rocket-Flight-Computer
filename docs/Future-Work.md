# Future Work

## The Next Steps

The future development of the ESP32 Rocket Flight Computer will focus on expanding system capabilities, improving reliability, and exploring autonomous aerospace applications.

## Short-Term Goals

The immediate goals for this project include:

- Fabricating the custom PCB
- Assembling the avionics hardware
- Developing comprehensive flight test procedures
- Performing the first live flight demonstration
- Successfully launching and validating the complete system

## Medium-Term Goals

Future improvements will focus on expanding the capabilities of the flight computer:

- Developing advanced sensor fusion algorithms
- Incorporating GPS-based location tracking into telemetry data
- Developing improved communication and telemetry systems
- Performing detailed analysis of live flight data

## Long-Term Goals

Long-term development may explore advanced autonomous flight capabilities, including:

- Autonomous attitude control and active stabilization
- Flight control systems for ascent and descent phases
- Autopilot capabilities, including waypoint navigation and loitering
- AI-enabled flight control systems

## Ultimate Goal

The ultimate goal of this project is to develop a research platform for studying autonomous vehicle systems and advanced aerospace control technologies.
